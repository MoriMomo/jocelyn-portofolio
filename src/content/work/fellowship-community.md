## Challenge

Initially, our high school had no formal student organization dedicated to spiritual life and service coordination, relying entirely on teachers for ad-hoc scheduling. Without clear roles or communication channels, student participation and continuity were limited.

## What I Did

I took the initiative to **establish and structure the school's Fellowship Organization**, building both its governance model and digital presence:

- **Organizational Architecture:** Scaled the community to **80 student members across 10 specialized divisions**, introducing brand-new functional teams including **Greeter, Social Media, Usher, and Writer** alongside Multimedia, Worship, and Sound & Lighting.
- **Operational Cadence:** Designed the end-to-end flow of worship services and instituted monthly evaluation meetings with all 10 division leaders to resolve bottlenecks and align execution with our vision and mission.
- **Digital Platform Creation:** Launched the school fellowship's first dedicated Instagram platform (`@sckfellowship.shs`), publishing service insights and community updates across more than 50 posts.

## Outcome

Transformed an informal, teacher-dependent activity into a self-sustaining **80-member, 10-division student organization** that broadened student participation and established a lasting leadership structure at the school.
