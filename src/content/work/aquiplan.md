## Challenge

Entering an international export market requires a product that solves a specific regional pain point while remaining durable across cross-border logistics. Targeting the Australian market, our team needed to design a practical plant-care innovation capable of withstanding extreme temperature conditions and conserving water.

## What I Did

Together with my teammate, I co-developed **AquiPlan** (*"Lazy pot can save water too"*), an export-oriented business innovation built for Australian households and commercial spaces:

- **Product Formulation:** Designed a self-watering planter constructed from **fiberstone**, combining structural durability under extreme heat with automated moisture regulation.
- **Market-Entry Strategy:** Positioned the product around water conservation and low-maintenance plant care tailored to Australian climate conditions.
- **Competition Pitching:** Prepared the commercial proposal and presented our export strategy across both the Indonesian national stage and the international finals.

## Outcome

AquiPlan earned **2nd Place at the National Level**, securing our selection to **represent Indonesia at the International Finals in Singapore**, where we competed against student delegates from across the Asia-Pacific region. This competition directly inspired my decision to pursue a degree in International Business Management and champion Indonesian enterprises on an international stage.
