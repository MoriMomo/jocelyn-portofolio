## Challenge

Delivering a meaningful youth development program at **Yayasan Sosial Tangan Kasih** required aligning 40 university students across multiple functional divisions—while remaining agile enough to handle real-time logistical and scheduling changes on the day of the event.

## What I Did

Serving as **Project Leader** for *The Little Visionaries: Building Tomorrow*, I directed the initiative from concept through on-site delivery:

- **Cross-Division Alignment:** Coordinated division coordinators, unified team objectives, and supervised preparation across program design, logistics, and participant engagement.
- **Curriculum Design:** Structured three interactive modules—career-sharing sessions, goal-setting activities, and creative tote-bag painting workshops—to help children explore their future aspirations.
- **Real-Time Crisis & Change Management:** When unexpected changes arose during live execution, I worked closely with division leads to adapt our original plans, reallocate available resources, and guide collective problem-solving on the ground.

## Outcome

The program ran smoothly and delivered an engaging, memorable experience for the children at Yayasan Sosial Tangan Kasih. Leading a 40-member organization under live operational constraints reinforced my approach to resilient, collaborative leadership.
