## Challenge

My high school previously lacked an entrepreneurship club or training ground to prepare students for business competitions. As a result, students interested in commerce had no structured guidance on how to analyze business cases or write competitive business plan proposals.

## What I Did

I took the initiative to **found the school's Business Club**, creating a peer-learning community dedicated to commercial analysis and competition preparation:

- **Club & Track Structure:** Organized a 7-group community spanning *Business Case Dissection (Bedah Bisnis)*, *Investment & Trading*, *Seminar & Workshop Information*, and competition study cohorts.
- **Proposal & Case Training:** Designed and taught practical sessions on how to structure a business plan proposal from problem statement to financial and market execution.
- **Peer Mentorship:** Equipped fellow students with the core evaluation criteria and presentation skills needed to enter external business plan contests.

## Outcome

Created a permanent space for aspiring young entrepreneurs at the school to dissect real-world business cases, build structured proposals, and confidently enter national student competitions.
