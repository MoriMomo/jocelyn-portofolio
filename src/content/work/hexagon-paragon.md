## Challenge

As consumer behaviors, digital touchpoints, and sustainability expectations shift rapidly among Gen Z buyers, **ParagonCorp** needed a cohesive strategic model to deepen brand affinity and connect its portfolio into an integrated ecosystem rather than isolated product lines.

## What I Did

Alongside my teammates on **Polisi Tidur Team** (EN Mobility, DWDG @BINUS), I co-developed the **HEXAGON Strategy** (*Harmonize, Engage, and eXpand Paragon*), structured around three core pillars:

- **Harmonize:** Designed the *Paragon Gen Z Beauty Universe*—an integrated beauty ecosystem built around Gen Z identity, self-expression, and daily lifestyle touchpoints.
- **Engage:** Formulated an omnichannel marketing strategy powered by community synergy to elevate two-way brand interaction, consumer participation, and long-term brand affinity.
- **Expand:** Outlined growth initiatives combining sustainable beauty solutions with authentic grass-roots community engagement to broaden Paragon's market reach.

## Outcome

Our pitch secured **3rd Place (#3 Winner)** in the **DWDG BINUS Internal Business Case Competition**, recognized for presenting a structured, consumer-centric roadmap for strengthening ParagonCorp's relevance with the next generation of Indonesian consumers.
