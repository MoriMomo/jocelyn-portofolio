## Challenge

High-volume coffee chains like **Kopi Kenangan** frequently experience peak-hour congestion and unpredictable in-store waiting times—friction points that erode customer satisfaction and slow down routine commuter orders.

## What I Did

Participating in the **Students x CEOs (Sxcelerate) Entrepreneurship Track**, our team developed the **Smart Queue & Routine Pick-Up Strategy** (*From Promo Choice to Routine-Ready Coffee*):

- **Digital Queue Allocation:** Designed a workflow enabling customers to claim virtual queue numbers online prior to arriving at the outlet.
- **Predictive Pick-Up Windows:** Integrated real-time estimated preparation and pick-up times so commuters can plan store visits around their daily routines.
- **Operational De-Bottlenecking:** Shifted order-status waiting out of physical store footprints to streamline barista throughput and counter handoffs.

## Outcome

Delivered a complete operational and customer-experience proposal for the Sxcelerate final capstone, demonstrating how lightweight digital queuing can convert promo-driven buyers into habitual daily commuters.
