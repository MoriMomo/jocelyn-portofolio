## Challenge

As an emerging startup, **BenihBunbun** sought to scale its commercial reach beyond individual consumers by building structured Business-to-Business (B2B) corporate partnerships. Accomplishing this required a clear consulting framework and tight alignment between the client's operational reality and strategic growth goals.

## What I Did

Entrusted with the role of **Project Manager** under **Do Well Do Good (DWDG) BINUS**, I oversaw project execution and multi-stakeholder collaboration from problem definition through final delivery:

- **Stakeholder Governance:** Managed communication workflows between the internal consulting team, project supervisors, academic advisors, and the BenihBunbun client team.
- **Business Problem Analysis:** Directed the diagnostic phase to identify key bottlenecks and growth levers in BenihBunbun's B2B value proposition.
- **Strategic Synthesis (ACE Strategy):** Consolidated analytical insights and advisor feedback into the **ACE Strategy** framework, outlining a repeatable roadmap for scaling BenihBunbun through corporate partnerships.

## Outcome

Delivered a comprehensive B2B strategic recommendation deck aligned with BenihBunbun's commercial objectives, establishing a structured partnership playbook while strengthening our team's consulting rigor under DWDG BINUS.
