## Challenge

Competing in the **BICON NATION (BINUS International Club Business Case)** competition, our team was challenged to elevate **Crystalin's** brand awareness among Gen Z consumers, convert digital attention into physical retail demand, and address new regulatory constraints affecting the brand's traditional mountain logo asset.

## What I Did

Together with my teammates, I developed the **C2M (Catch the Moment, Create the Memory)** marketing and brand strategy:

- **Microdrama Storytelling & Funnel Architecture:** Designed short-form microdrama content tailored to Gen Z media habits, paired with tiered influencer collaborations across **TOFU, MOFU, and BOFU** stages to move audiences from awareness to purchase.
- **Demand-to-Store Strategy:** Built an activation bridge linking digital campaign engagement directly to product pull at offline retail outlets.
- **Visual Identity Prototype:** In response to regulatory changes around Crystalin's mountain imagery, we prototyped an alternative visual identity highlighting the **crystal** element as a distinctive, compliant brand asset.

## Outcome

Competing in our **very first business case competition**, our team secured **5th Place** at BICON NATION, validating our integrated approach to digital storytelling, retail conversion, and regulatory-ready brand design.
