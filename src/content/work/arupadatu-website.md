## Challenge

During my final year of high school, my team and I completed an internship at **PT Arsitek Arupadatu**, an architecture and engineering consultancy, where we were challenged to diagnose and solve an operational bottleneck. We identified that the firm's company website had fallen out of date because internal staff lacked the time and specialized web-development expertise required to maintain it.

## What I Did

Focusing on long-term operational usability rather than technical complexity, our team designed a practical digital publishing workflow:

- **Operational Diagnosis:** Assessed the firm's content update frequency and internal technical constraints to choose a platform the staff would actually sustain.
- **User-Friendly Web Design:** Designed and structured a clean company website and architectural portfolio in Canva, complete with service overviews and project showcases.
- **Handover for Autonomy:** Configured the template architecture so non-technical team members could independently update project portfolios and company information without external developer dependencies.

## Outcome

Delivered a maintainable, cost-free digital presence that enabled PT Arsitek Arupadatu to keep its public portfolio current using existing internal resources.
