## Challenge

To stand out in a crowded merchandise market, **SahabatBulu (`@sahabat.bulu.id`)** needed stronger product differentiation and deeper community retention tailored to how Gen Z consumers express personal identity.

## What I Did

As **Project Leader for Business Development & Marketing Strategy**, I guided our six-person team through research, product line architecture, and go-to-market planning:

- **Market & Consumer Intelligence:** Conducted market research, competitor benchmarking, and primary consumer surveys to uncover unmet demand for personalized, identity-driven merchandise.
- **Product Line Innovation:** Formulated three targeted merchandise lines—the **Identity Collection**, **Food Lover Collection**, and **Positive Mindset Collection**—aligned with Gen Z self-expression trends.
- **Engagement & Commercial Mechanics:** Designed customer retention programs centered on audience **co-creation**, **limited-edition releases**, and **pre-order systems** to build anticipation and support sustainable sales growth.

## Outcome

Produced a data-driven business development and marketing roadmap for SahabatBulu that connected primary survey insights directly to new collection concepts and community-led launch mechanics.
